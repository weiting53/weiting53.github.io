PRJ-012025 – NOW
WINDING DOWN
ROLEMASTER'S THESIS · SOLE DEVELOPER

Bedtime scrolling is hard to stop with hard blocks and reminders. Winding Down makes the phone gradually less stimulating as bedtime approaches. I built the whole Android system — screen overlays plus on-device network traffic shaping through VpnService — and ran a multi-week field deployment with real users.

  • Android
  • Kotlin
  • VpnService
  • Field study
  • Behavior change
